### I would be precise about the novelty claim

I would **not** write:

> **There are no data on earmarking.**

Because your own literature review identifies things such as:

- Cashin et al.'s literature-assembled health-earmark database covering more than 80 countries;
- WHO country examples;
- road-fund inventories and operational studies;
- individual Ghana/South Africa/etc. cases. 

So the much stronger and more defensible claim is:

> **To our knowledge, there is no representative cross-country legal inventory that identifies the prevalence and statutory design of revenue earmarking across SSA.**

**No denominator.**

**No comparable source–destination mapping.**

**No common taxonomy.**

That precision actually makes the novelty claim more credible.
